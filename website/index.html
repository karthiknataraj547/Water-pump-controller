Architecture & Innovations

Why HydroPulse Leads Industrial IoT Water Control

Built from the ground up for high reliability, zero false-online states, and instant responsiveness.

⚡

Sub-100ms Ping Latency

Strict hardware request-response handshake. The app verifies live ESP32 presence with active ping/pong polling and never relies on ambiguous MQTT broker connections.

🌊

3D Volumetric Fluid Visuals

Interactive 360° rotatable solid cylindrical tank with real-time sinus wave oscillations, cascade overflow pipelines, and rotating motorized impellers.

🛡️

Dual-Layer Fail-Safe Logic

Local FreeRTOS safety engine executes on the ESP32 hardware independently of Wi-Fi or cloud outages to prevent dry runs, overflows, and pipe bursts.

📶

Zero-Touch BLE Provisioning

Native Bluetooth Low Energy discovery wizard connects to unconfigured ESP32 controllers in seconds and securely transmits Wi-Fi credentials with GATT handshakes.

🔄

In-App Automatic OTA Updates

Integrated version check engine queries GitHub releases on app launch and notifies users of new firmware & app builds with one-tap APK installation.

📊

Historical Telemetry & Analytics

Full daily, weekly, and monthly trend breakdowns of power consumption (kWh), water pumped (Liters), and relay actuation cycles.
